About

Physics → materials → quantum technology.

I am an M.S. student in Materials Science and Engineering at Duke University with a background in physics from UC Berkeley.

My work combines experimental and computational approaches to understanding low-dimensional materials. I am particularly interested in the physics of two-dimensional interfaces, spin-orbit phenomena, quantum devices, and emerging semiconductor technologies.

Beyond my current research, I am interested in the intersection of quantum science, semiconductor technology, and computational methods for designing future electronic systems.
