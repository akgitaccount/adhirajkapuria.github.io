Undergraduate Research / Advanced Experimental Physics Projects

Advanced experimental physics projects

Experiments from UC Berkeley's advanced physics laboratory, each a measurement of a foundational effect.

Hall effect in a plasma

Measurement of the Hall effect in an ionized gas, extracting transport properties of the plasma from its response to crossed electric and magnetic fields.

CO2 laser

Operation and characterization of a carbon-dioxide gas laser, examining its emission and the molecular transitions responsible for it.

Rutherford scattering

Measurement of the angular distribution of alpha particles scattered from a thin metal foil, reproducing the signature of Coulomb scattering from a compact nucleus.
